# Functional Requirements

## Student Management
- The system shall allow students to create accounts.
- The system shall authenticate registered students.
- The system shall allow students to update their information.
- The system shall provide password recovery.

## Accommodation
- The system shall display available hostels.
- The system shall display available rooms.
- The system shall display room occupancy.
- The system shall allow students to submit booking requests.
- The system shall allow authorized staff to allocate rooms.
- The system shall support student transfers.

## Payments
- The system shall display hostel fees.
- The system shall process hostel payments.
- The system shall generate payment confirmations.
- The system shall maintain payment history.
- The system shall identify outstanding payments.

## Maintenance
- The system shall allow students to report maintenance problems.
- The system shall allow students to attach supporting information.
- The system shall track maintenance requests.
- The system shall allow maintenance staff to update request status.

## Security
- The system shall allow students to register visitors.
- The system shall allow security officers to verify visitors.
- The system shall record visitor entry and exit.
- The system shall maintain security records.

## Communication
- The system shall allow students to submit complaints.
- The system shall allow administrators to manage complaints.
- The system shall allow administrators to publish announcements.
- The system shall send relevant notifications.

## Administration
- The system shall allow authorized staff to manage hostels.
- The system shall allow authorized staff to manage rooms.
- The system shall maintain student accommodation records.
- The system shall generate hostel reports.
- The system shall provide occupancy information.

# Non-Functional Requirements

## Security
- User accounts must be protected from unauthorized access.
- Sensitive information must be protected.
- Staff access must be controlled according to user roles.

## Performance
- The system should respond quickly to normal user requests.
- Room availability should be updated accurately.

## Availability
- The system should be accessible to students and authorized staff when required.

## Usability
- The interface should be simple and easy to understand.
- Students should be able to complete common tasks without unnecessary steps.

## Reliability
- Important transactions such as bookings and payments should be recorded accurately.
- The system should prevent duplicate or conflicting room allocations.

## Maintainability
- The system should be structured so that individual components can be updated without disrupting the entire system.
